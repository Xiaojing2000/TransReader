# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

TransReader is built for thoughtful reading, and its community should feel equally thoughtful.

译页服务于专注阅读，我们也希望社区交流同样专注、友善、具体。

We welcome people of every background and level of experience. Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than identities, assume good intent while still addressing real impact, and make room for newcomers to ask basic questions. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of another person's private documents or credentials are not acceptable.

我们欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者。请尊重他人、讨论观点而不是身份、在重视实际影响的同时善意理解，并允许新参与者提出基础问题。骚扰、歧视、威胁、性化关注、人肉搜索，以及公开他人的私人文档或凭据，均不可接受。

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers; do not start a public pile-on.

维护者可以为保护社区而编辑或移除内容，并临时或永久限制参与。行为问题请私下报告给维护者，不要组织公开围攻。
